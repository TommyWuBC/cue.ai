Cue privacy details

Cue starts on a shopping tab only after you choose “Start on this page.” On approved stores it resumes as you navigate until you say “Cue, exit.” Looking at a product does not click or buy it.

Camera and gaze

Webcam frames are processed on your device to estimate where you are looking. Cue does not send video frames to its server. The eye tracking models are packaged with the extension. Cue keeps reduced eye calibration samples in Chrome extension session storage for the current tab so you do not need to recalibrate after opening another page on the same store. This data is removed when you exit Cue or close the tab.

Speech and product information

Cue sends microphone audio to the configured Cue server for transcription. Depending on its configuration and availability, the server may send that audio to xAI or ElevenLabs. Cue sends your spoken request, the selected product’s details, nearby visible product details, and the current page URL to the Cue server to answer your question or perform a requested action. If Grok is configured, the server may send the request and product context to xAI. Response text may go to xAI or ElevenLabs for speech generation. Provider availability is shown by the Cue server’s health endpoint.

Storage and orders

Products discussed for comparison are kept in Chrome extension session storage for the current tab, not the store page’s storage. This memory is removed when the tab closes and expires after one hour. The demo store keeps approved orders, passkeys, and budget records in the Cue server’s local SQLite database. It caches generated speech audio on disk. The current checkout records demo orders and does not charge a card.

Choice

You can stop Cue on a shopping page by saying “Cue, exit,” revoking camera or microphone permission in Chrome, or removing the extension. The Cue server operator controls its API keys, logs, cached audio, and demo order database.
